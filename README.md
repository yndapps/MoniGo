 MoniGo — Privacy Policy

 1. Introduction

MoniGo (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a personal finance and money-management application designed to help users record and organize their financial information.

This Privacy Policy explains how MoniGo accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application and its available features.

 2. Information We Handle

The information processed by MoniGo depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and the services you connect.

 2.1 Financial Information

You may provide information such as:

- Income, expenses, transfers, balances, and transaction dates.
- Transaction descriptions, categories, and payment methods.
- Budgets, savings goals, emergency funds, and financial plans.
- Debt, loans, assets, investments, and other financial records.
- Notes, photographs, and attachments associated with transactions, where supported.
- Financial records associated with additional user profiles you create.

This information is used to provide financial tracking, calculations, summaries, reports, reminders, and related app functionality.

 2.2 SMS and Transaction Detection

If you enable SMS-based transaction features and grant the necessary permission, MoniGo may access SMS messages relevant to identifying financial transactions.

The feature may process information such as transaction amounts, dates, merchant or sender details, account references, and transaction descriptions to identify and prepare transaction records.

Automated detection may be incomplete or inaccurate. You should review the generated transactions before relying on them.

SMS permissions are used for the transaction-related functionality described above, not for selling message contents or creating advertising profiles.

Data handling clarification: The final implementation must specify whether SMS contents are processed entirely on your device, whether any message content is transmitted, and whether message text is retained after transaction extraction. The app's actual behavior, in-app disclosure, and Google Play Data safety declaration must agree with this Policy.

 2.3 Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If you choose to connect a Google account for sign-in or backup functionality, MoniGo may process the Google account information and authorization permissions necessary to provide that feature.

Depending on the feature, this may include account identifiers, basic profile information made available through the authorization flow, access tokens handled by the relevant authentication components, and Google Drive file or folder information.

MoniGo uses the applicable Google authorization to provide the requested functionality. It does not use Google account access to collect unrelated information.

Google Drive backup is intended to store supported MoniGo backup files in a folder named “MoniGo,” subject to the permissions and behavior of the implemented backup feature.

Google processes information under its own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

Google Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

 2.4 Voice Input

If you use voice-based transaction entry, MoniGo may use microphone access to capture your spoken input for conversion into transaction information.

The exact information processed and whether audio or speech data is sent to an external speech-recognition provider depend on the speech-recognition technology used by the app.

We will disclose any relevant external processing before or when the feature is used, as required by applicable law.

 2.5 Photographs and Attachments

If you attach photographs or other files to financial records, MoniGo processes those files to provide the requested attachment feature.

Depending on the implementation, attachments may be stored on your device and may be included in backups if the backup feature supports them.

You should avoid attaching unnecessary personal information, identity documents, or other sensitive material.

 2.6 Device and Technical Information

The app or its supporting services may process technical information necessary for security, compatibility, and operation, such as app version, operating system version, device-related identifiers, diagnostic information, and error reports.

Whether diagnostic, crash-reporting, analytics, or other SDK data is collected depends on the third-party libraries included in the released app. Any such collection must be reflected in this Policy and in the Google Play Data safety declaration.

 3. How We Use Information

We use information only for purposes related to providing, maintaining, protecting, and improving MoniGo, including:

- Recording and organizing transactions and financial records.
- Calculating totals, balances, budgets, trends, and reports.
- Managing goals, debts, assets, reminders, and other financial features.
- Detecting potential transactions from SMS messages when enabled.
- Processing voice input and attachments when requested.
- Creating and restoring backups when you choose to use those features.
- Authenticating Google account access where necessary.
- Verifying purchases and providing paid Smart features.
- Diagnosing technical errors and protecting the app against misuse.
- Responding to support requests and meeting applicable legal obligations.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate legal basis and any required notice or consent.

 4. Where Your Information Is Stored

MoniGo is designed to manage financial records through the app on your device and, when you choose to use supported features, through external services such as Google Drive.

The precise storage location depends on the feature:

- On-device records: Financial data may be stored in the app's local storage.
- Google Drive backups: Backup files may be uploaded to your authorized Google Drive account when you initiate or enable backup functionality.
- Third-party processing: Certain information may be processed by authentication, billing, speech-recognition, diagnostic, or other service providers when those services are used.

We do not claim that all information always remains on your device. The released app's implementation determines which information is transmitted to external services.

 5. Backup Security and Encryption

MoniGo may provide backup encryption to help protect financial information stored in backup files.

The level of protection depends on the implemented encryption method, key generation and storage, backup process, and restoration process.

Access to a Google Drive account does not necessarily guarantee that a backup can be decrypted or restored. Restoration may fail if backup files are corrupted, required credentials or keys are unavailable, or the app's restoration requirements cannot be met.

You should protect your device and Google account, maintain access to any required recovery credentials, and periodically verify that important backups can be restored.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We do not sell personal financial records or SMS contents to advertisers.

Information may be processed or shared in the following limited circumstances, where applicable:

Service providers: Google and other providers may process the information necessary to provide services you request, such as sign-in, Drive backup, payment processing, or speech recognition.

Legal requirements: We may disclose information when required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from an authorized authority.

Security and misuse: We may process or disclose relevant information when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, enforce our legal rights, or protect the security of MoniGo, subject to applicable law.

Business transfers: If the business operating MoniGo undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of assets, information may be transferred where legally permitted and subject to appropriate safeguards and notices.

We do not authorize service providers to use your personal financial information for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to MoniGo, except where otherwise permitted by law.
