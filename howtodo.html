MoniGo — User Guide & FAQ

Tap a category to expand questions, then tap any question to read the answer.
Transactions & Entry Features ▼
How to use voice entry? +
Simply speak about your transaction. It will automatically detect the Type, Amount, and Category. For example, say "movie 500" and it will record Expense: Movie 500.
How to add recurring transactions? +
Tap + on the Dashboard to open the Add Transaction page. Near the Time field, tap the recurring icon and fill in the details regarding repetition schedule, reminders, etc.
How to bulk edit or delete multiple transactions at once? +
On the Transaction screen, long-press any transaction to enter bulk selection mode. Select multiple items, then use the top action bar to change Category, Pay Mode, Move Account, or Delete in one tap.
How does automatic SMS scanning work in MoniGo? +
MoniGo scans incoming bank SMS messages to auto-detect transaction amount, pay mode, and due dates. It creates transaction drafts that you can review and approve in one tap.
How to use Split in transactions? +
When adding a transaction, tap Split to divide a single payment across multiple categories or subcategories (or import directly from a Shopping List). MoniGo automatically calculates remaining amounts so your category spending breakdown stays exact.
Debt & Balance Management ▼
What does the Yellow icon near amount do? +
It is the Clear Debt button, used to quickly fill in the remaining balance required to settle a debt.
What is the difference between Lent and Borrow in Debt Manager? +
Under Debt Manager, "Lent" tracks money you gave to others (which they owe you), while "Borrow" tracks money you took from others (which you need to pay back).
What is the difference between Real Balance and Spendable Balance in Pay Mode? +
Real Balance is Cash + Bank - Credit Card Debt ± Debt balance. Spendable Balance subtracts Emergency Fund target, Goal dues, and Recurring expenses from Real Balance so you know what money is safely available.
How do Credit Card statement and payment due reminders work? +
In Pay Mode, set your card's statement and due dates (or let MoniGo auto-extract them from Bank SMS). MoniGo sends reminder alerts before your due date so you never miss a bill.
How to share Debt details with someone? +
Open Debt Manager and select the person's debt account. Tap the Share icon at the top right of the debt card to generate and send a statement image and text summary via WhatsApp, Email, or messaging apps.
Budgeting, Goals & Planning ▼
How does Money Move help me? +
Money Move compares your Actual Income against your Planned Budget to help you achieve zero-based budgeting. It categorizes spending into Essentials, Lifestyle, and Investment so you can maintain a healthy financial ratio, while tracking your Income Covered percentage and remaining Buffer.
How to create and track financial Goals? +
Set a target savings amount and completion date for goals (e.g., New Car or Vacation). Record contributions over time, set reminder notifications, and track your progress percentage.
How does MoniGo calculate my Emergency Fund target? +
MoniGo analyzes your average essential monthly expenses and multiplies it by your chosen safety duration (e.g., 3, 6, or 12 months) to set your recommended emergency target.
What does the Green icon in the Budget limit dialog do? +
It is the Copy from Last Month button. Tapping it automatically copies all category budget limits set in the previous month into your current month's budget fields.
Reports & CSV Import/Export ▼
How to export PDF or CSV financial reports? +
Go to Tools > Reports, select your export format (PDF or CSV), filter by Date Range, Categories, Pay Modes, or Amount Range, and tap Export to save or share your report.
What happens to existing data during CSV import? +
By default, new transactions are merged into your existing account data. If you enable "Discard existing data" in Import Settings, MoniGo will clear old transactions for that account before importing.
How does MoniGo handle new or unrecognized Categories and Pay Modes in the CSV? +
MoniGo opens a guided Category & Pay Mode Mapping screen. It automatically matches identical names and lets you manually merge items, rename them, or assign Category Purpose (Essential, Lifestyle, Investment) and Pay Mode Type (Bank, Card, Cash, Wallet).
Backup, Security & Sync ▼
How to backup and restore financial data securely? +
Go to Settings > Backup & Restore to export AES-encrypted local backup files, or enable automatic encrypted sync to your personal Google Drive.
How does backup password / encryption key work? +
Backups use AES-256 encryption. If using Google Login, the key links to your account for automatic restore across devices. If using an offline App Key, save your key (e.g., MG-XXXX-XXXX); entering it during restore decrypts your data on any device.
